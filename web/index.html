A REVIEW-FIRST WORKFLOW

Performers → Photos ✳

Stage your videos by performer, then review them in Google Photos.

01

Scan & identify in Stash

Add your source and destination (or their parent) as video library folders above. Scan your source, open Stash, and identify/edit each scene's performer and title. This workflow waits for your metadata; it doesn't identify scenes for you.

Before moving: keep a separate backup of important videos. Stash's automatic database backup is not a video backup. Keep other Stash clients and file operations idle.
02

Preview the rename & move

Choose a source and a destination in shared phone storage. The destination picker can create a new folder inside an existing Stash library. Performer subfolders will be created within it.

Names: performer / scene title [file ID].ext. One performer per video; subtitle sidecars stay behind. Only same-volume moves are offered.

Review changes

Uncheck videos to exclude them. Resolve multi-performer scenes below.

DRY RUN

Stash backs up its database first, then moves files one at a time. Do not write to these folders concurrently; the destination check is not atomic.

03

Back up, album & review

In Google Photos, turn on backup for each new performer folder. Verify every video's Details says Backed up in the right account; create/add it to the matching performer album. Folder names do not create albums. Confirm both checks below.

Help Photos discover videosOpen Google Photos

Android assistance is untested on your device. Discovery covers up to 500 recent local workflow videos. On a browser, open Google Photos yourself. Backup quality and any unlimited entitlement are controlled by Google Photos.

Moved videos and your review checklist will appear here.

For cloud-only cleanup: after both checks, open that specific video in Photos and use Delete from device / Remove from device. Do not use Trash or broad “Free up space”. This app never deletes videos. Once the local copy is gone, Stash cannot play it until downloaded again; don't run Stash's Clean task without understanding its effects.

ANDROID STORAGE

Connect your storage

Android needs your permission before Termux can see shared folders.

  1. Open the Termux app on your phone.
  2. Run the command below and approve Android's permission prompt.
termux-setup-storage

If access is still denied, check Android Settings → Apps → Termux → Permissions. Some Android versions also require “All files access”.

Some app-private folders and SD cards remain restricted by Android. The browser only shows folders Termux can read.

GOOD TO KNOW

Here if you need us.

A few practical notes for running Stash on your phone.

Keep Termux running

Android may stop background processes or restrict battery use. Keep Termux open and consider disabling battery optimization for it.

Folders & permissions

Only folders Termux can read are shown. This is a file-path browser, not Android's native folder picker.

Your existing library

The managed server uses ~/.stash/config.yml and its existing database. Folder changes replace Stash's configured list only when you press Save. Back up important data first.

Local, not invisible

The managed server is bound to this device. Other apps on the same phone may still reach local services. If another Stash process is already running, its own network settings apply.

Stop the companion

This closes the setup server and any Stash process it started. Other Termux sessions are not closed.

Need more?

Check the activity log or open the upstream Stash documentation.
